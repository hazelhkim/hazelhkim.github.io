Reasoning Acquisition from Limited Sources
  • Models reason well with quality, abundant data, and give biased perspectives with scarce, poor data.
  • I study how models acquire reasoning ability from a limited quantity or quality of resources.
Causal Inference, not Correlation
  • Correlations limit reasoning about causes not present in the text.
  • I study causal inference to control language models against correlation-biased data.
Information Quantification
  • Usable information varies with how input and output interact.
  • I study information quantification that interprets model behaviors.
Emergent Knowledge of Language Models
  • As scale grows, models emerge knowledge smaller models do not.
  • I study whether that knowledge is trustworthy for transparent, real-world use.
